# Store release notes — 1.4.3

The two stores are at different versions, so "what changed" differs. Paste the
matching section into the **Release Notes** field.

---

## Firefox / AMO (published version is 1.4.1)

A new icon, and a fix.

The extension no longer does any work while it is switched off. Turning it off
used to leave the part of it that watches for audio running in tabs that were
already open — it kept analysing whatever those pages played, until the page was
reloaded. Off now means off, everywhere, immediately.

The icon is new: two waveforms, one above the other, which is what the extension
is for — one recording against another.
